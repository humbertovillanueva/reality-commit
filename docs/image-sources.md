## Evidence

The matching housing is a curated interpretation of the same contributor's pump-repair series and visible geometry. No serial-number identity has been verified. P-001 is an illustrative ID. Different camera positions and disassembly are not treated as damage or removal events.

Displayed dates use source EXIF calendar dates: February 11 and February 18, 2009. The after image's primary date field is malformed (0002). Source clocks are not independently verified. Internal noon values are date-only placeholders, not asserted capture times. No made-up 2026 visits, readings, repair costs, service status, or failure probabilities are supplied.

The source author reports cleaning and residual pitting. Our observations describe visible deposits and exposed uneven metal. They do not establish pitting depth, cause, remaining wall thickness, or fitness for service. The author's sealing report is not a test certificate. The sample review is educational and manually authored, not an AI diagnosis.
